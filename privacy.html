Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

This site is a personal portfolio for Ranel Andrew Zantua. It's a static site with no account system, no server-side database, and no form that submits your information anywhere. This page explains what little data is involved when you visit.

What this site collects

This site itself does not collect, store, or sell any personal data. Specifically:

  • There are no cookies set by this site.
  • There is no analytics or tracking script running on this site.
  • There is no contact form. The "Email me" button opens your own email app via a mailto: link; nothing is sent through this site or to me unless you choose to send that email yourself.

Local storage

This site saves one small preference in your browser's local storage: whether you've chosen dark or light theme. That preference stays on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Third-party services

This site loads two fonts (IBM Plex Mono and IBM Plex Sans) from Google Fonts. Loading a font from Google's servers means your browser makes a request to Google, which can see your IP address and basic request information, the same way it would for any site using Google-hosted fonts. See Google's privacy policy for details on how Google handles that data.

This site also links out to GitHub, GitHub Pages, and LinkedIn for project demos and contact purposes. Those are separate services with their own privacy policies, and visiting them is governed by their own terms, not this one.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Standard web server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and request time) may be collected by GitHub as the hosting provider, the same way any web host logs requests. I don't have access to or use that data myself. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Certificates

The certificates shown on this site were issued to me by TESDA (the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority) and are displayed here for portfolio purposes. TESDA, not this site, is the authoritative source for verifying them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date above.
